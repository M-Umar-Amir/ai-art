FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to send for a quote?

Drawings or CAD files are ideal, but a sketch, a sample part or a description is enough to get started. Include material and quantity if you know them.

Do you handle prototypes as well as production?

Tell us what you need — one-offs, short runs or ongoing supply — and we'll recommend the right approach.

Can you help if my part needs design changes?

Yes — raise it in your request and the team will flag anything that would make the part easier or cheaper to manufacture.

How do I get started?

Use the quote form on this page or call us directly.
