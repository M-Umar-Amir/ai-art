`models/free-curated/SOURCES.json`; that file records model names and SHA-256.
`LICENSE.txt` preserves its CC0 statement. Official Food/City/Racing archive
downloads and their license files are preserved under `models/free-assets`.

The ten custom courier and environment items are generated by
`models/generate_courier_collectibles.py` using free Blender. This is procedural
mesh generation, not AI inference; no paid service or third-party source mesh
was used. `models/free-curated/GENERATED.md` lists these separately from CC0 files.

## Existing assets retained

- Original Ace, Nova and Bolt GLBs, including native textures. Never substitute generic people.
- Previously generated neon blocks, skybridge, rooftop tower/apartment, boards,
  delivery pod and food market; their Blender generation scripts are preserved.
- Free Quaternius city kit and nature models and Poly Haven street furniture already imported.
- Detailed authored cones, roadworks, cargo gate, lamp and planter meshes.
- Branded signs, holograms, festival motifs and route/rooftop structural geometry.
  These are authored compositions, not missing-model fallback assets.

Roads, floor slabs, lane paint, support bounds and UI panels remain code-native
geometry. Replacing these with unrelated decorative models would change course
dimensions or UI layout. They still need visual polish against the PDF.

## Verification and limits

Actual generated boots, Magnet Pack and Shield Drone have been rendered and
inspected; previews live in `review/free-assets`. Validation checks that all live
crew, pickup, obstacle and power-up slots reference meshes. Build and in-game
capture results must be recorded after completion in WORKLOG.md.

This audit does not establish that the entire environment matches the PDF's
premium visual target. Festival compositions, rooftop layout, materials,
lighting, motion and mobile performance still need separate acceptance. Free
asset acquisition is not evidence of visual fidelity.
