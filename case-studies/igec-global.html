Case Study · Education · Website Redesign

IGEC Global

A complete redesign for an Australia-focused education and student visa consultancy — almost 30 years of experience, finally presented with the clarity and confidence it deserves.

UX StrategyWebsite RedesignFront-End BuildAI-Stylised ImageryBilingual EN / ARAccessibility

The Challenge

IGEC Global has guided students to Australian universities for almost three decades, with recognised certifications and a strong track record. Its website needed to communicate that trust instantly and turn visitors — many of them students and parents comparing several agents — into booked consultations.

Our Approach

We rebuilt the site around a single journey: understand why Australia, see how IGEC helps, find a course, and book a free consultation. A course finder sits right in the hero, trust signals come immediately after, and the consultation call-to-action is always one tap away.

The redesign also adds an English / Arabic toggle, an accessibility toolbar, a live chat entry point, and AI-stylised campus and city imagery that gives the site a consistent, premium look.
