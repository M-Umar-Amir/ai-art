Case Study · Food & Beverage · Digital Foundation

Happy Ice Cream

Four decades of Karachi heritage, brought up to speed online — product photography, heritage campaign concepts, a website redesign and a sprint plan covering Google, delivery apps, social and paid ads.

Product PhotographyAI-Enhanced VisualsCampaign ConceptsWebsite RedesignGoogle & Delivery AppsSocial & Ads

The Challenge

Happy Ice Cream has been part of Karachi since 1982, with several established branches, a proven menu of ice creams, faloodas and shakes, and a loyal customer base built over generations. The brand was never the problem — its digital presence simply hadn't kept pace.

The website looked dated, there was little professional photography of the products, Google listings were under-developed, there was no paid advertising, and social posting was inconsistent next to newer competitors.

Our Approach

We designed a focused digital foundation sprint, with photography as the anchor. Every downstream channel — Google, Foodpanda, Indolj, social and ads — is built from one consistent set of visuals. We then restructured the plan around the Sharfabad branch to put the budget where it drives visibility and orders most directly.

Client
Happy Ice Cream, Karachi
Heritage
Since 1982 · multiple branches
Engagement
Digital foundation sprint, revised to 7 phases around Sharfabad
Visual scope
40 hero images · 15 short videos · 8 cinematic reels
Channels
Google Business · Foodpanda · Indolj · Instagram · Facebook · Meta & Google Ads
01

Product Photography

Signature sundaes, faloodas, shakes and scoops, shot and AI-enhanced into a consistent library of hero visuals: rich chocolate on dark backdrops, strawberry on blush pink, and colourful falooda in motion.

02

Campaign Concepts

Theme explorations built on what makes the brand irreplaceable: its heritage. “Happiness Since 1982”, “The Taste You Grew Up With” and “Some Memories Never Melt” — three generations sharing the same ice cream, alongside bold product-led social creatives.

03

Website Redesign

A full redesign mockup for the existing Indolj ordering site: heritage storytelling up front, the menu presented as appetising product cards, WhatsApp ordering and a branch locator — handed over so the live site could be rebuilt to match.

04

The Sprint Plan

Seven phases, each with a clear, checkable output — and a week-by-week roadmap so the client always knows what is happening next.

  • 01 · PhotographyOne on-site day at Sharfabad: 40 hero images, 15 short videos, 8 cinematic reels.
  • 02 · Google BusinessProfile optimisation, weekly posts and review management.
  • 03 · FoodpandaMenu optimisation with new photography, descriptions and offers.
  • 04 · IndoljOrdering-site optimisation: images, descriptions and offers.
  • 05 · Social12 posts, 8 reels and stories across Instagram and Facebook.
  • 06 · AdsMeta and Google campaigns with geo-targeting around each branch.
  • 07 · ReportingWeekly updates and a final performance report.
  • Measured, not assumedAnalytics and Search Console set up on day one, with a joint review at the end of the sprint.
