Case Study · Fashion · AI Brand Film

Zahra Noor

A Garden Above the Clouds — one floral three-piece suit, turned into a dreamlike 15-second brand film and a full set of vertical social edits, without a location shoot.

Creative DirectionAI Talent & StylingLocation DesignStoryboardBrand FilmSocial Cutdowns

The Brief

Zahra Noor needed a launch film for a new floral printed three-piece — kurta, trousers and dupatta — that felt premium, feminine and aspirational. The garment had to stay exactly true to the real product: the print, the scalloped lace hems, the flower buttons and the tasselled dupatta.

Our Approach

We treated it like a real fashion production, just without the logistics. The actual garment was the source of truth. We locked a consistent model, designed a world around the print's palette, storyboarded every second, and then generated and edited the film in both landscape and vertical formats.

The result is one campaign world — a marble terrace floating above a sea of clouds — that works on YouTube, Instagram Reels, Stories and paid social.

01

The Product Is the Source of Truth

Everything starts from a clean studio flat of the real garment. Every later frame is checked against it, so the print scale, lace trims, button clusters and dupatta border never drift.

02

Designing the World

A location sheet defines the set before a single shot is generated: a white marble terrace with neoclassical arches, blossom canopies and flowering urns, floating above the clouds in soft golden daylight. The palette is pulled straight from the print.

Blush Pink Ivory Warm Cream Soft Green Sky Blue Marble Beige
03

Every Second Storyboarded

Fifteen seconds, five beats. Each beat has one job — from the first glimpse of wonder to the closing brand portrait with the Zahra Noor mark.

The Film

Built for Vertical
