FAQs

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Email hello@dhqglobal.co and the team will get back to you.

Why is branding important for my business?
Before you consider marketing and sales, branding is the core foundation of your business. It creates an identity your customers recognise and build a loyal relationship with. Without a brand identity, marketing efforts fall short of connecting.
What is the difference between branding and logo design?
Logo design is one part of your brand development. Branding includes a brand guideline that sets out the concept, font styles, colour selections and theme, plus brand kits that show how your logo looks on merchandise, stationery and other items.
How much does it cost to design a logo?
Standard pricing for a single logo starts from $50. We can confirm exact pricing once we know the design and the number of logos you need.
What is the process of logo design?
After our first conversation we send you a logo design requirement-gathering form to learn as much as we can, then present three options to choose from. We can make further tweaks and adjustments to the one you select.
Do you offer training after building a WordPress, Shopify or e-commerce site?
Yes. Training is as essential as the website itself. Without it, you wouldn't be able to understand and manage your site.
What do you consider when developing an e-commerce website?
Product categorisation, web security, PCI compliance, CMS integration, SEO, mobile responsiveness, multiple payment options and shopping cart design.
What types of Google Ads campaigns do you offer?
Our Google Ads service covers the search network, video and the display network.
How long should a whiteboard animation be?
An effective whiteboard animation is 90 seconds or less. We take it from script and storyboard through visuals, animation, music, sound and voice-over.
Why do colours on screen look different from printed colours?
Monitors and printers use different colour models: screens use RGB and printers use CMYK. CMYK can match up to about 90 percent of RGB colours, depending on the equipment used.
