Why Australia

A world-class education, and a life to go with it

Australia consistently ranks among the world's top study destinations — here's what that means for you.

  • World-class universities

    Home to several of the world's top-ranked universities, with strong industry links across every major field.

  • Post-study work pathways

    Eligible graduates can access post-study work visa options — we'll help you understand what applies to your course.

  • Safe, welcoming cities

    Multicultural campuses and cities that consistently rank among the most liveable in the world.

  • Work while you study

    Most student visa holders can work part-time during term, helping offset your living costs.

  • Vibrant student life

    From Sydney's harbour to Melbourne's laneways, campus life comes with genuinely great cities attached.

  • Scholarships available

    University, government and country-specific scholarships can significantly reduce your costs — we'll help you find them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need IELTS or PTE to study in Australia?
Most universities require proof of English proficiency such as IELTS, PTE Academic or TOEFL. Exact score requirements vary by course and institution — our counsellors will confirm exactly what your target universities need.
How long does the student visa process take?
Processing times vary by nationality, application volume and how complete your documents are. We'll walk you through the checklist and give you a realistic, personalised timeline.
Can I work while studying in Australia?
Most student visa holders are permitted to work part-time during term and additional hours during scheduled breaks, under current Department of Home Affairs rules. We'll confirm the latest limits that apply to your visa.
What does it cost to study and live in Australia?
Tuition and living costs vary widely by city, course level and lifestyle. We'll help you build a realistic budget and explore scholarship options to help offset the cost.
Can IGEC help me find scholarships?
Yes — our counsellors help you identify university, government and country-specific scholarships you may be eligible for, as part of your overall application strategy.
What happens after I graduate — can I stay and work?
Australia offers post-study work visa pathways for eligible graduates. Eligibility depends on your course, provider and visa subclass — our team will map out your options as part of your planning.
