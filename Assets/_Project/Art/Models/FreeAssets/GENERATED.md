# PDF-specific generated courier assets

Source: generate_courier_collectibles.py, using free Blender 4.5.15.
These are newly generated meshes made from the local script, not downloaded assets
and not AI inference. They use no third-party meshes or textures. The original
Ace, Nova and Bolt GLBs are untouched.

free-magnet-pack.glb: U-shaped yoke, pole caps, coils and pack.
free-gravity-boots.glb: paired high-top boots with magnetic heel and sole details.
free-speed-core.glb: contained accelerator core and cooling rings.
free-dash-battery.glb: battery housing, charge cells and terminals.
free-shield-drone.glb: four ducted rotors, sensor and courier housing.
free-courier-chip.glb: metal chip, processor and pins.
free-double-core.glb: paired score cells.

Kenney files are separately listed in SOURCES.json and licensed by LICENSE.txt.

free-roof-vent.glb: fan grilles, intake louvres and service access panel.
free-roof-tank.glb: vessel, support legs, reinforcing bands and ladder.
free-festival-stage.glb: deck, trusses, speakers, LED backdrop and spotlights.
